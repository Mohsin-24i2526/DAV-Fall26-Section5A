Lab 09: Hierarchies, Networks and Maps

Data Analysis and Visualization · D3.js · Interactive visualizations

Task 1 — Course Enrollment Treemap

Rectangle area represents student enrollment. Colors show departments. Hover over a course for details.

Task 2 — University Structure Dendrogram

University → departments → courses. Click a department node to collapse or expand its courses.

Task 3 — Flight Route Network

Drag cities to reposition them. Thicker links mean more weekly flights. Hover over a city to highlight its routes.

Task 4 — World Population Choropleth

Countries are colored by population in millions. Gray means no population row was supplied. Hover to see details.

Task 5 — Pakistan Cities Point Map

Circle size represents population in millions. Only cities above 1 million are labelled; hover to see every city.

Task 6 — Combined Spatial Encoding Map

Position = location; circle size = population; color = hub (4 or more routes); line width = weekly flights. Hover over a city to highlight its connected routes.
